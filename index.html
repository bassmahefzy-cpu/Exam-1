Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about buying, selling, and investing in real estate

How do I know if I'm ready to buy a home?

You're ready to buy a home when you have stable income, a good credit score (typically 620+), a down payment saved, and you plan to stay in the area for at least 3-5 years. Our team can help assess your financial readiness and connect you with mortgage services.

What's the best time of year to sell my home?

Spring and early summer are traditionally the best times to sell, as buyers are more active and homes show better with good weather. However, local market conditions and your personal timeline are more important than the season.

How much should I offer on a house?

Your offer should be based on comparable sales in the area, the home's condition, current market conditions, and your budget. Our agents will provide a detailed comparative market analysis.

What are closing costs and who pays them?

Closing costs typically range from 2-5% of the home's purchase price and include fees for loan origination, appraisal, title insurance, attorney fees, and taxes. Both buyers and sellers may have closing costs depending on the agreement.

Should I get a home inspection?

Yes, absolutely. A professional home inspection can identify potential issues before you buy and may save you thousands of dollars. We recommend inspections for every property type.

How long does it take to buy or sell a home?

For buyers, the process typically takes 30-45 days from offer acceptance to closing. For sellers, the average time on market varies by location and price range.

What makes EliteHomes different from other real estate companies?

We combine modern technology with personalized service, offering virtual tours, property matching, market analysis, and a dedicated team of real estate experts for every client.
